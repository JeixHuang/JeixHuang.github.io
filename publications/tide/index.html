TIDE: Temporal-Aware Sparse Autoencoders for Interpretable Diffusion Transformers in Image Generation

Victor Shea-Jay Huang* (equal contribution), Le Zhuo* (equal contribution), Yi Xin, Zhaokai Wang, Fu-Yun Wang, Yuchi Wang, Renrui Zhang, Peng Gao, Hongsheng Li

AAAI ↗ arXiv ↗ Citation ↘

TL;DR

Temporal-aware sparse autoencoders reveal hierarchical concepts across diffusion timesteps, making diffusion transformers easier to interpret and control.用时间感知的稀疏自编码器提取扩散各时间步中的层次化概念，理解并控制 Diffusion Transformer 的生成过程。
