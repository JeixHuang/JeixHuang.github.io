Driving Intents Amplify Planning-Oriented Reinforcement Learning

Hengtong Lu* (equal contribution), Victor Shea-Jay Huang* (equal contribution), Chengmin Yang, Pengfei Jing, Jifeng Dai, Yan Xie, Benjin Zhu* (equal contribution)

arXiv ↗ Project ↗ Citation ↘

TL;DR

Intent-conditioned proposals and multi-intent GRPO expand and preserve diverse driving maneuvers for preference-based reinforcement learning.通过意图条件采样与多意图 GRPO，扩展并保留多样驾驶行为，为基于偏好的强化学习提供更丰富的候选轨迹。
